About Sunrise Smoothie Co.

Sunrise Smoothie Co. is a fast-growing cold-pressed juice, smoothie and acai-bowl franchise founded in 2014. With 220 locations across 18 states, the brand is known for clean ingredients, a compact footprint that fits high-traffic transit hubs and malls, and a semi-absentee ownership model that lets new owners ramp without quitting their day job.

Franchisees receive a three-week training program, a protected territory, supplier relationships, and grand-opening marketing support. The brand reports average gross margins around 58% and a typical ramp to profitability of 4–6 months.

Pros & cons

What owners love

  • Built-in foot traffic in malls & transit hubs
  • Semi-absentee — keep your day job while you ramp
  • Strong ~58% gross margins reported by franchisees
  • Three-week training & grand-opening support

Things to weigh

  • High up-front capital vs. home-based options
  • Lease & location quality make or break it
  • Seasonal dips in colder-climate markets
  • Royalty + marketing fees (6% + 2%)
